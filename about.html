Our Story

Luxury Hair & Beauty in Chelsea Since 2016

Established 2016

The Art of Refined Beauty

Founded with a vision to combine modern hair artistry with restorative care, Luminar Beauty Studio has become one of Chelsea's most trusted boutique sanctuaries. We believe that visiting a salon should be more than a simple transaction; it is a vital ritual of self-care and artistic rejuvenation.

Our philosophy is grounded in precision and customisation. Every consultation is detailed, ensuring that our work highlights your personal style, skin tone, and features. From master-crafted hair coloring to advanced skin health rituals, we balance high-end artistry with gentle organic wellness practices.

Core Pillars

Our Brand Values

01. Expertise

We are dedicated to ongoing craft training. Our stylists stay ahead of modern trends in London and Paris to deliver world-class technical precision.

02. Luxury

From the peaceful mews surroundings to the rich organic formulas and individual consultation booths, every detail is engineered for premium comfort.

03. Care

We believe in sustainable, long-term health for your hair and skin. We customize our treatment methods to match your specific biological profile.
